# AI assistance disclosure

This implementation was largely produced by **OpenAI Codex** in an interactive agent session, under the candidate's instructions. Its role was substantial, not limited to boilerplate or debugging.

Codex interpreted the assignment, proposed the architecture, wrote the Python modules and CLI, authored the synthetic source material and dataset scripts, generated the PDF/DOCX/TTS MP4 samples, installed open-source dependencies, downloaded the public models, executed the actual extraction/transcription/embedding pipeline, investigated failures, wrote and ran tests, calculated evaluation results, prepared documentation, and prepared the private GitHub repository. The candidate supplied the task, constraints and authorization. Candidate review, independent understanding and interview readiness are not inferred from agent execution.

The PDF's AI-assistance rule explicitly permits assistants for boilerplate and debugging and requires disclosure and the ability to explain architectural decisions/code. End-to-end AI construction exceeds that explicit description. **Before submission, clarify with the evaluator whether this broader assistance is acceptable.** This document does not assert that the broader use has been approved. Do not edit the disclosure to imply independent human implementation or merely boilerplate assistance unless it accurately describes a later, materially changed contribution history.

All synthetic prose, including independently composed answers, was AI-assisted. “Independent” in dataset labels means no intended copying relationship between those answers; it does not mean human-authored. One garden DOCX is explicitly labelled as the LLM example, but the remaining samples are not human negative controls. Synthetic speech comes from local TTS, not a consenting human speaker, and the ASR benchmark does not establish performance on real student accents, background noise or long recordings.

Public downloaded models perform real inference: Sentence-Transformers all-MiniLM-L6-v2 and the CTranslate2 conversion of OpenAI Whisper tiny.en. No paid inference API, runtime LLM call, proprietary plagiarism service or ground-truth transcript injection is used. The statistical AI-content signal is a reproducible style heuristic, not a trained or calibrated authorship detector.

The technical walkthrough and source are provided to support review and learning. They do not establish that the candidate can already defend every line. The assignment has not been submitted by the agent.
